# Product Guidelines - Malgo

## Prose Style & Voice
- **Technical & Formal:** Documentation should be professional, clear, and logically structured, suitable for a technical and research-oriented audience. Use precise terminology and avoid conversational filler.
- **Academic & Rigorous:** Maintain a tone that reflects the project's foundation in PL research. Explanations should be thorough and grounded in formal concepts.

## Branding & Visual Identity
- **Tone:** Academic and rigorous. Malgo should be perceived as a serious tool for exploring language design and intermediate representations.

## UX Principles
- **Clear Error Feedback:** The compiler and interpreter must provide detailed and actionable error messages to help developers quickly diagnose and fix issues.
- **Predictable Pipeline:** The compilation process should be logical and transparent, with a clear flow between different intermediate representations (Fun, Core, Flat, Join).

## Naming & Terminology
- **Pragmatic & Familiar:** Use a mix of established academic terms from functional programming and category theory with pragmatic, descriptive names that are easy for developers to grasp.
